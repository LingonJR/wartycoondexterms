Acceptance

By adding War Tycoon Dex to a server, joining our support server or using its commands, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use the bot. You also need to follow the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

About the bot

War Tycoon Dex is a fan-made Discord bot where you catch, collect and trade War Tycoon vehicles as cards. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox, Discord or the developers of War Tycoon.

Rules

1. Death threats

Sending death threats or telling other players to "kys" over cards or trades is strictly prohibited.

2. Exploitation

Using bugs, exploits, automation, or manipulating the bot to gain an unfair advantage is forbidden and may result in a permanent blacklist.

3. Farms

Farms are not allowed. See Farms below for what counts as one.

4. Harassment and hate

Harassment, hate speech and doxxing are not allowed, in trades or anywhere else the bot is used.

5. Scamming

Don't mislead people in trades, for example by lying about a card or pretending to be staff.

Farms

No farms may be used with War Tycoon Dex.

A farm is a server made purely for collecting War Tycoon Dex collectibles that has fewer than 10 members. A small server where people actually talk and hang out is not a farm. One that exists only to spawn and catch cards is.

If we find a farm, we can remove the bot from it, blacklist the server and its owner, and take back cards that came from it.

Cards and trading

Cards only exist inside the bot. They have no value in the actual War Tycoon game, because the bot is not synchronized with the vehicles in the game. Owning a card gives you nothing in War Tycoon, and what the bot shows can differ from what is in the game.

Cards can't be sold for real money or Robux. However it can be traded between other dex bots, if both parties agree.

Data

We store your Discord user ID, your collection, your trades, the IDs and basic settings of servers the bot is in, invites as described above, and moderation records such as warnings and blacklists. We don't sell your data. To ask for your data to be deleted, contact us below. Blacklist records may be kept so banned users can't come straight back.

Enforcement

Breaking these terms can lead to a warning, removal of cards, a temporary or permanent blacklist, or the bot being removed from your server. For serious cases such as threats or exploitation we may act without warning.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows when they last changed, and using the bot afterwards means you accept the new version. The bot is provided as is, and we aren't responsible for lost cards or downtime.
